Résumé (PDF)

I’m a designer and a word nerd. I specialize in conversational AI, and in helping agents stick to the voice and tone targets a team sets. I design an assistant’s persona and how it talks with people, then build the standards and measures that keep it clear, trustworthy and on brand. My other specialties are trust and safety, experimentation and helping people get together. I taught college writing and poetry first, and I’m a teacher at heart.

Experience

Meta

Senior Content Designer, AI Lead · Nov 2022 to May 2026

Verso

  • Initiated and built Verso, a conversational AI agent that grew from one product area to teams across Meta in about six months. Designed its persona and interaction principles: it asked what a product actually did before suggesting wording, then worked through options with content designers and gave engineers a strong recommendation that still required content approval. Verso →
  • Built the engine behind it as an MCP server in Python, so Verso could test its own draft against a team’s standards and revise it before anyone saw it. That self-check made it follow rules more consistently than any other design agent at Meta.
  • Defined each product’s voice and tone as a measured fingerprint, built from the words that product had already shipped, starting with Meta Horizon, then Instagram, WhatsApp, Facebook and Meta Quest. With the fingerprint, Verso followed a product’s rules much more accurately than an agent working from a prompt file alone.
  • Consolidated scattered content standards into one structured index and turned every rule with a yes or no answer into a check. When someone challenged a standard, Verso drafted the change for the lead content designer who owned it, a review that ironed out inconsistencies over time.

Meta Horizon product content in VR and on mobile

  • Led content design on social coordination initiatives, helping people get together across VR and mobile. Redesigned the Meta Horizon portal, the doorway between worlds, so people knew where they were going and who was there before they stepped through. It debuted at Connect 2024, and the portal experiment increased monthly and weekly active users about 7 to 8%. Developed just-in-time education principles for first-run VR with the onboarding team, and the new experience increased first-world visits about 13%. Portals → Meta Horizon NUX →
  • Led content design on trust and safety initiatives as Horizon Worlds became Meta Horizon, from moderator screens to recording disclosures. Worked with legal and localization partners to replace six separate notice sequences with one coordinated approach. Work samples →
  • Led experimentation for content designers across Meta Horizon and Meta Quest, building the practice for 35+ designers with workshops, templates and shared trackers. Their results were featured in both company-wide experiment roundups that year.
