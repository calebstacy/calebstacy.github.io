Product screens from Meta Horizon

These screens show my content design work across Meta Horizon. They cover trust and safety, social coordination, and mobile onboarding. Each group keeps the screens with the situation they address. Select an image to read it at a larger size.

Trust & safety

The moderator screens here were part of the trust and safety work I did when Horizon Worlds became Meta Horizon. The Meta Horizon NUX article also covers my work on recording disclosures.

Social coordination

Helping people get together, which Meta called social coordination. Most of these screens are for the moments when joining someone doesn't go as planned.

Meta Horizon mobile

I worked on the first choice people saw when they opened the Horizon app. The way we asked it could send someone setting up a headset down the wrong path.

A question about setup

The earlier screen asked people to choose a device. Research found that some people interpreted the phone option as casting from a headset. I changed the question to “Are you setting up a headset?” so the choice addressed what someone was trying to do. The setup question performed better in testing.
