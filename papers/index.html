Papers

Four studies, re-set in one document register so a reader who hasn't seen the underlying work can travel fast or check closely without it being two different documents.

  • Working paper · Governance Governing a measured voice

    A rulebook for what a piece of writing is allowed to sound like, tested against real numbers, including one rule that had to be narrowed the day better evidence came in.
  • Working paper · Measurement Netflix and Discord, measured

    Two companies' public writing, measured and compared properly: where they genuinely differ, and where they don't. In a few places, there simply wasn't enough text collected to know either way.
  • Working paper · Naming grammar A naming grammar for Tudum headlines

    The reusable templates behind most Tudum headlines, mapped out and checked against Discord's blog as a control for what's actually specific to Netflix.
  • Working paper · Product naming How Netflix names its shelves

    Netflix's public shelf and genre names follow four rules consistent enough to check a draft against. The paper reads them out of the shipped catalog, exceptions included, and writes them down as records.
